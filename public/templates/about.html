The Beginning

Born in the Heart
of Naples

It all began with Nonno Giuseppe, a third-generation pizza maker in the Spaccanapoli quarter of Naples. In 1987, he packed his secret dough recipe and brought authentic Neapolitan pizza to the world.

Today his grandchildren run the restaurant with the same passion, the same wood-fired oven, and the same uncompromising respect for ingredients that made Nonno famous in his neighbourhood.

  • Certified by the Associazione Verace Pizza Napoletana
  • All dough fermented for minimum 48 hours
  • No freezers — everything fresh, every day
  • Seasonal menu that changes with the local market
  • Family-run for three generations

Our Story

Passion Baked Into
Every Slice

Born in Naples, our story began in a small kitchen where Nonno Giuseppe perfected his dough recipe over decades. Three generations later, we bring that same soul to every pizza we make.

  • Imported "00" flour from Naples
  • San Marzano tomatoes from Mount Vesuvius
  • Fresh mozzarella di bufala, hand-pulled daily
  • Dough fermented for minimum 48 hours
